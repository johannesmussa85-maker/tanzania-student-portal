Application checklist

A simple sequence to reduce avoidable mistakes.

01 · VerifyCheck the institution and programme with the relevant regulator.
02 · ReadReview current entry requirements and the institution’s admission notice.
03 · PrepareKeep accurate certificates, identification details and other required documents ready.
04 · ApplyUse the institution’s authorised application route and keep your reference details.
Trust rule: TCU states that undergraduate applicants apply directly to the higher education institution of their choice, and the institution administers its admission procedure. Always check the current official notice before paying fees or submitting personal information.
