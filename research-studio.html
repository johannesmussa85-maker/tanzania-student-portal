Source quality checklist
  1. Prefer peer-reviewed papers, academic books, official datasets and credible institutional sources.
  2. Record author, year, title, publication and link/identifier while researching.
  3. Compare evidence instead of collecting sources without synthesis.
  4. Match every important claim to an appropriate source.
  5. Use one citation style consistently.
