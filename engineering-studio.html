Engineering Calculators

Triangle Area

Area = ½ × base × height.

○

Circle Area

Area = πr².

Ω

Ohm's Law

Current = voltage ÷ resistance.

⚡

Electrical Power

Power = voltage × current.

💧

Flow Velocity

Velocity = flow rate ÷ area.

⚖️

Density

Density = mass ÷ volume.

Mining Engineering

Mining & Mineral Processing

Useful workflows for grades, recovery, mass balance thinking and comminution.

MINING
🥇

Metal Recovery

Recovery = contained metal in product ÷ contained metal in feed × 100.

⛏️

Contained Metal

Contained metal = tonnes × grade fraction.

⚙️

Reduction Ratio

Reduction ratio = feed size ÷ product size.
