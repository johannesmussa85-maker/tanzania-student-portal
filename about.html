About the portal

Tanzania Student Portal

A student-focused resource hub that brings study materials, exam preparation, books, courses, career tools and opportunity links together in one place.

What we provide

The portal organizes useful starting points for secondary and advanced study, university and college research, digital books, course exploration, CV preparation and student opportunities.

Built for students

The design aims to keep important resources easy to find on both desktop and mobile, with simple navigation and clear guidance.

Our source approach

Official providers should be used for authoritative information. The portal also links to third-party educational resources where they can help with study and practice.

What we do not claim

The portal does not claim ownership of external files or third-party websites. Availability, admissions rules, exam information and opportunities can change, so users should verify details with the relevant provider.
